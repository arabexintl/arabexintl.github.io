Questions

Certificate translation FAQ

Which certificates and documents can you translate?

Any document. Educational certificates, mark sheets and transcripts, birth and marriage certificates, experience and salary letters, licences, legal papers and anything else you need translated.

Which languages do you translate between?

We translate from Arabic into any language, and from any language into Arabic — for example English or Malayalam to Arabic, and Arabic to English.

How quickly will I receive my translation?

We offer fast delivery. You will get a clear delivery time together with your quote, before any work begins.

How do I send my certificate?

Send a clear photo or scan on WhatsApp, or email it to arabexinternational@gmail.com. Tell us the target language and where you will submit it.

How much does certificate translation cost?

Every document is quoted individually. You receive a fixed price before we start — no hidden fees.

Do you attest or issue certificates?

No. Arabex translates documents only. Attestation, legalisation and the issuing of certificates are done by the relevant authorities.
